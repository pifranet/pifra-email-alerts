PIFRA Email Alerts for Your Monthly Salary Slip: How They Work

Most government employees do not miss their salary — they miss the slip that proves it. The email alert is the small message that tells you the new slip is ready, and PIFRA email alerts for salary slips are the reason you no longer have to visit an accounts office or wait for a printed copy to reach your department.

What the alert actually is

The alert is not the slip itself in every case, and it is not a login dashboard. In practice, once your email is registered against your personnel record, the monthly payroll run triggers a message to that inbox. That message either carries the slip as an attachment or tells you it is available, depending on the cycle. Either way, the alert is your cue to save the document the same day — not a message to leave unread for weeks.

The habit that matters: when the alert arrives, download and rename the slip immediately (for example, PIFRA-slip-2026-10.pdf). Employees who do this never have to reconstruct a year's slips in June.

Step 1 — Register the right email, once

Alerts only go to the email attached to your record, so this step decides everything that follows. Use a Gmail address you actually open every day, not an inbox you created years ago and forgot. Registration asks for your government code, personnel number, 13-digit CNIC, date of birth in DDMMYYYY format, and an active mobile number. Enter them only on the official m.pifra.gov.pk portal — check the address bar shows a .gov.pk domain before typing anything personal.

Step 2 — Know when to expect the alert

Alerts follow the payroll run, not a date you can set your watch by. Expect them around month-end and the first days of the new month. If you registered mid-month, do not expect an alert for that same month: the first alert normally arrives with the next payroll cycle after registration. Marking that expectation in advance saves a lot of unnecessary worry.

Step 3 — Check the slip, not just the alert

An alert arriving is not the same as a correct slip. Open the attachment and glance at four things: basic pay and allowances against your current scale, deductions such as income tax and GP fund, any arrears or adjustment lines, and personal details like name spelling, designation, and DDO code. A sudden jump in a deduction, or a missing allowance, is worth a question to your accounts office while the month is still fresh.

Alert not arriving? Troubleshoot in order

  1. Spam folder first. The first alert is the one most likely to be filtered. Mark it "not spam" so later months land in the inbox.
  2. Mailbox full or old email? If you changed your email or stopped opening an inbox, the alert may be going somewhere you no longer check.
  3. Departmental transfer? After a transfer, confirm with the new DDO's accounts office that your email is still attached to the personnel record — the first slip after a transfer is the one most likely to go missing.
  4. Registration details wrong? A wrong government code or a date-of-birth mismatch against the service record causes silent failures: no error message, just no alert.
Protect your data: your slip contains a CNIC-linked payroll record. Never forward it to unofficial WhatsApp groups or "salary slip checker" pages — the only place your CNIC belongs is the official .gov.pk portal.

Quick questions

Do alerts cost anything? No. Registration and the monthly slip are both free — anyone charging a fee is not official.

Can I get alerts for older months? The email service covers the ongoing monthly cycle going forward. Older slips come from your accounts office, so start the archive now.

Is there an official app instead? No official app is needed — the email service is the channel. Anything calling itself a PIFRA slip app outside .gov.pk should be treated with suspicion.

Where can I read the full background? The main resource hub is pifraregistration.net — registration, email setup, DDO codes, and troubleshooting in one place.
